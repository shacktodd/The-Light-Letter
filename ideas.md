# Oracle Engine — Design Exploration

## Three approaches considered

### 1. The Marginalia Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** An archival research instrument styled as a field notebook with graphite, ochre, and document-blue annotations. It makes uncertainty feel tangible rather than decorative.

**Probability:** 0.06

### 2. The Evidence Observatory
**Very Brief Intro:** A sober scientific control room with dark panels and luminous signals, treating every claim as a reading under calibration. It emphasizes navigation through competing confidence levels.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. The Cabinet of Friction
**Very Brief Intro:** A neo-editorial broadsheet where assertions, counterarguments, and gaps physically interrupt one another. Its energy is investigative and deliberately un-smooth.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach — The Marginalia Ledger

**Design Movement:** A contemporary interpretation of archival annotation, scientific field notebooks, and late-modern editorial design.

**Core Principles:** Evidence has hierarchy, so original sources must be visually separable from inference. Uncertainty is an object, not a footnote. The interface should reward slow reading while making comparison fast. Every visual accent must carry a research function.

**Color Philosophy:** Warm archival paper and graphite establish patience and seriousness; a mineral-blue annotation color signals verified source trails; oxidized orange flags unresolved or speculative material. The contrast is intended to make calibration emotionally legible rather than merely technical.

**Layout Paradigm:** A left reading rail provides persistent orientation, while the main content shifts between wide editorial cards and narrow marginal notes. Claims are organized as a vertical dossier, not a conventional dashboard grid.

**Signature Elements:** Margin brackets that bind a claim to its audit; confidence stamps with tactile ink-like outlines; a small “denominator field” visualization that exposes counted and uncounted terrain.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interaction is investigative rather than ornamental. Expanders disclose audit details, filters compare confidence tiers, and source links open as clear research exits. Controls should feel like handling a dossier.

**Animation:** Entrance movements are short (180–240ms), opacity-and-transform only, and staggered lightly down the research stack. Audit cards should unfold from the linked claim edge. Reduced-motion mode removes all nonessential movement.

**Typography System:** Fraunces for the analytical display voice; IBM Plex Sans for dense evidence and controls; IBM Plex Mono for IDs, tiers, and source apparatus. Headlines use expressive serif weight; evidence stays compact and high-legibility.

**Brand Essence:** An adversarial research atlas for readers who want the fascinating claim and its brake system in the same frame. **Skeptical, precise, curious.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are specific and question their own seductions; CTAs invite inspection rather than conversion. Example lines: “Awe enters here; the denominator enters next.” and “Open the evidence trail, not just the headline.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A geometric black-and-blue “O” formed from an open circular bracket and a central calibration dot—an orbit that does not close until tested.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Cobalt Annotation — #2558C8.**
